# Shure-SM57-Repair

## Behavior before repair
- The microphone didn't have an output
- The top of it with the little mesh as pop filter was spinning like crazy
- It looked great otherwise

## Disassembly
The first thing I had to do was to disassemble the entire thing. There are plenty of videos of how to do this thing and I strongly recommend anyone who is going to repair or take apart the microphone to watch one, because the internals are really delicate. Especially the diaphragm with coil.
The microphone is composed of two main parts. The bottom with the XLR connector and the top part or body with the cartridge inside. The bottom part can be easily unscrewed but since our problem is not in the connection to the connector itself, we leave it as it is. The top part is more painful and I'm not going to explain how you should disassemble it. Just look at the tutorial.

## The problem and it's repair
when i first opened the cartridge, the thin diaphragm with its little coil was falling off. It was entirely separated from the body with magnet so I had to be careful. Then I scraped the old glue off of the cartridge body and glued back the diaphragm to the body using super glue. I don't think this is the right glue I should be using but let's see how long it'll last before breaking again. So far so good

before reattaching and glueing the coil, you need to make sure there isn't any dust or otherwise the coil will rub against the magnet. Speaking of this, another tough thing you have to do is getting the coil somehow exactly in the middle of the magnet, which isn't an easy task.
